## Contribute To SiberianCMS

The best way to help SiberianCMS is to tell us what does not work.

## Submit An Issue

Open an issue on [github.com/Xtraball/Siberian/issues](https://github.com/Xtraball/Siberian/issues). Search the existing issues first, yours may already be there.

Describe your problem and give us:

- your Siberian version,
- what you did, what you expected, what happened instead,
- a screenshot whenever possible.

We are not taking pull requests, forks or code contributions: issues are all we need.
